How to Play

1. Drop & Match: Direct horizontal & vertical pairs (like two 8s) merge immediately. Clusters of 3+ (L-Shape/T-Shape) merge cleanly into one higher number.

2. 3 Lives System: Each lost life automatically refills in 15 minutes.

3. Save Your Game: When a column fills up, watch a quick ad to clear the top 2 rows and keep playing!

4. Power-ups: ↩️ Undo takes back your last drop (1 free each game). 🔨 Smash removes any tile you tap.

5. Daily Gift: Come back every day for a bigger gift. Day 7 also refills your lives!
