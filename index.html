How to Play

1. Tap or swipe: Choose any lane to drop the number block.

2. Smooth Physics: Watch blocks accelerate and drop down.

3. Instant Merge: When two matching numbers collide, they collapse into one higher block (2+2=4, 4+4=8).

4. Overfill: Don't let the blocks reach the top danger line!
