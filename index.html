How to Play

1. Tap any column: The top number tile drops into that track.

2. Vertical & Horizontal Merges: When matching numbers touch vertically or horizontally side-by-side, they merge together into their sum (2+2=4, 32+32=64).

3. Keep Space: Don't let columns fill to the top!
