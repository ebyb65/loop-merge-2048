Privacy Policy for Drop 2048

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Drop 2048 collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Drop 2048 does not collect or require any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, address, or phone number) to play the game.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Drop 2048 is free and shows ads (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads). Ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) and the advertising networks it works with, including Unity Ads. These services may collect and share information from your device to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (for example, the Google Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from your IP address)
  • App interactions with ads and diagnostic information
  • Information about apps on your device, such as which app stores are installed

These services are governed by their own privacy policies: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

3. Your Ad Choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks whether you agree to personalised ads before ads are loaded. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change your choice at any time from the pause menu under Privacy Choices.

On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Local Storage

The application stores gameplay progress (such as your high score, remaining lives, refill timers, power-ups, daily gift streak and your ad choice) locally on your device. This information is not sent to us.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our game is not designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Security

We value your trust. Because no sensitive personal data is collected or stored remotely by us, your personal information remains secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us through our developer page on the Google Play Store.
