## X2 Blocks (Inspired Square FZE)

- **Size of the target**: 4.9 from about 80K ratings (US page); 4.7 from 978 (Kuwait). Age 13+. Latest version 4.4.0 (24 Oct); every release note says only "UI Improvements".
- **Pitch**: "Merge, Drop & Reach Infinity". Blocks go "1024 -> 2048 -> 4096 and so on till Infinity". Listing bullets: free, minimal design, simple controls, easy to learn hard to master, auto save, global leaderboards, "No time limits", "No wifi connection required", haptic feedback.
- **Onboarding**: not described on the page. One reviewer found the next-number preview confusing.
- **Level design**: endless single board, no levels. A reviewer asks to start from bigger numbers; the developer's reply says the game removes old small numbers as bigger ones are made, and offers a restart at a larger number after a game ends.
- **Economy**: diamonds (earned by playing, more by watching ads, or bought). Reviews mention diamonds buy help such as swapping/removing a block. IAPs: diamond packs $0.99-$99.99, "AdFree Forever" $9.99, a $3.49 monthly subscription, "X2 Premium" $19.99.
- **Ads (from reviews)**: the main complaint by far.
  - "about 15 of 30 minutes were ads", ad breaks "roughly every 2 minutes", ads hard to close, push to the Play Store, frequent prompts to rate and claim prizes (Dec 2022).
  - "if you play 30min around 20min of them is ads" (Jan 2022).
  - Ads still shown after buying ad-free (Oct 2022).
  - Political campaign ads during the 2020 US election made a player delete the app (Jan 2021). The developer blamed third-party networks.
  - The developer's own reply: they "will change ad frequency if it affects users too much".
- **Retention features on the page**: global leaderboards only. No daily challenge, missions, events or themes are listed.
- **What players praise**: simple, relaxing, "no clock", "makes you think but also relax", "Addictive".

## Drop the Number: Puzzle Merge (A2 Apps)

- 4.6 from 103 ratings, no update since Sep 2022. Pitch: "fun and relaxing number match game", play online or offline, leaderboard, compare numbers with friends, "Buy gold to get extra lives and get past 2048!".
- IAPs: Remove Ads $2.99, coin packs, bundles up to $74.99.
- Reviews: "Ads never go away" (banner stayed after paying to remove ads), "Just ads and nothing.", "Super addictive."

## What we can learn (without copying)

1. **Ad load is the genre's number-one complaint.** Both games' negative reviews are about ads, and X2 Blocks' cadence (a break about every 2 minutes) is close to ours: Drop 2048 shows a timed mid-game interstitial after 135 s of play (ads-controller.js), with a 3 s warning. The CEO decided on 23 Nov to keep it, so no change is proposed here; this is evidence for Omar to weigh when real session data exists. Things we already do better: 45 s grace before the first ad, a warning toast, rewarded videos optional.
2. **A next-block preview is standard in drop-merge games, but it must be readable.** Drop 2048 has no "Next" preview today. Showing the next block, clearly labelled, lets players plan; X2's reviewer shows an unclear preview confuses people. (Backlog: d-next-preview.)
3. **"Reach infinity" is the long-term goal players chase.** X2's headline and the restart-at-a-bigger-number requests show players care about the big tiles. Our tiles above 2048 still fall back to the "2" colour (d-tile-colors), which makes our own "keep going" promise look unfinished. Raise its priority.
4. **Paid ad removal must remove every ad, including banners.** Both games got 1-star reviews for banners after paying. Not relevant until we sell "remove ads", but it is the rule when we do.
5. **Block sensitive ad categories.** Political ads cost X2 a player. Our games are rated for everyone; LevelPlay/Unity let publishers block ad categories. (Backlog: ad-categories, Omar, needs a CEO approval because it is an ad-network account setting.)
6. **Rate prompts and "claim prize" pop-ups annoy.** If we add a rating prompt, ask once, after a good moment (a new best score), never after a loss.
7. **Listing angles that work for everyone in the genre**: relaxing, no time limit, plays offline, haptics. Our approved Drop 2048 listing already says offline and no time limit.

## Not copying

X2 Blocks' name, "X2" branding, diamond economy and look stay theirs. The ideas above are generic genre mechanics (next preview, higher tiles, ad hygiene).
